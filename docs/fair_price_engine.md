# Fair-Price Intelligence Engine (`FAIR_PRICE_ENGINE_V1`)

SIH 26090 — Artisan Market Linkage & Smart Seller Matching

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The Fair-Price Intelligence Engine (`FAIR_PRICE_ENGINE_V1`) is a deterministic, evidence-based economic decision support module designed for Indian artisans. Rather than relying on black-box machine learning models or arbitrary statistical estimations, it synthesizes:
1. **Artisan-Provided Production Economics**: Itemized raw materials, skilled labor hours, labor rates, packaging, freight, and allocated overhead.
2. **Source-Backed Market Evidence**: Documented retail and wholesale price benchmarks from official and verified institutions (such as TRIFED / Tribes India, Central Cottage Industries Emporium, and ODOP registries).
3. **Traceable Mathematical Explanations**: Ordered, step-by-step transparency linking every rupee in the recommended range back to verified inputs.

---

## 2. Five Strict Information States

To ensure data integrity and avoid misleading artisans, the platform enforces strict segregation across five information states:

```
[ ARTISAN_PROVIDED ]  --> Raw materials, labor hours, wage rates, overhead, desired margin
[ SOURCE_BACKED ]    --> Documented market observations from official registries
[ AI_SUGGESTED ]     --> Staged heuristic or NLP tags (never canonical prices)
[ CALCULATED ]       --> Deterministic mathematical baselines from FAIR_PRICE_ENGINE_V1
[ HUMAN_CONFIRMED ]  --> Explicit review and confirmation by the artisan
```

1. **`ARTISAN_PROVIDED`**: Information directly entered by the artisan regarding their product costs, batch sizes, and profit targets.
2. **`SOURCE_BACKED`**: Market reference prices retrieved from genuine public registries, government bodies, or verified institutional catalogues.
3. **`AI_SUGGESTED`**: Suggestions generated by AI assistants (such as descriptive text); AI is strictly prohibited from generating canonical price figures.
4. **`CALCULATED`**: Mathematical derivations computed exclusively by the backend using Python `Decimal` arithmetic.
5. **`HUMAN_CONFIRMED`**: Data explicitly reviewed and accepted by the artisan. **Note**: Human confirmation indicates artisan approval; it is never represented as government certification or statutory endorsement.

---

## 3. Strict No-Fabrication Policy

The system adheres to an uncompromising no-fabrication standard:
- **No Synthetic Market Benchmarks**: If fewer than 3 comparable documented observations exist ($N < 3$), the engine **never** generates a synthetic median or simulated competitor price.
- **Explicit Insufficient Evidence State**: The engine returns `INSUFFICIENT_MARKET_EVIDENCE` and defaults to a transparent **Cost-Only Baseline**.
- **No Deceptive Terminology**: Calculated outputs are never labeled as *"Guaranteed Fair Price"*, *"Correct Market Price"*, or *"Official Government Price"*. They are clearly presented as an *"Evidence-Based Fair Price Analysis"*.

---

## 4. Minimum Observation Threshold & Comparability Framework

For market evidence to be incorporated into a fair price range:
1. **Craft Match**: Observations must belong to the exact same craft identifier (`craft_id`).
2. **Currency Consistency**: All values must be in Indian Rupees (`INR`).
3. **Material Compatibility**: Observation primary materials must match or be compatible with the craft's authentic raw materials.
4. **Freshness**: Observations older than 24 months are flagged as `STALE` and degraded to `LOW` evidence quality.
5. **Sample Threshold**: At least $N \ge 3$ valid, non-duplicate, fresh observations must be present. If $N < 3$, market statistics are suppressed.

---

## 5. Calculation Versioning

All price analyses store the calculation version:
- **Current Version**: `FAIR_PRICE_ENGINE_V1`
- **Future Changes**: Any future adjustments to margin curves or statistical methods will increment the version (`FAIR_PRICE_ENGINE_V2`), ensuring historical calculations remain 100% reproducible and auditable.
